Swordpile — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 October 2026

Swordpile ("the app") is made by Anvilbyte Studios (operated by the developer), which is the controller of the personal data described here. Contact: anvilbytestudio@gmail.com.

The short version

You play without an account, and your progress stays on your device. Google AdMob shows ads and processes device data to do so. Google Play or the App Store handles purchases. Two optional features use a server we run on Supabase in the European Union: Rankings, only if you tap Join, and a purchase check, only in versions where we have switched it on. We do not sell your personal information and do not use it for personalised advertising.

On your device

Your save, your settings and a list of the products you own are stored on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them. The in-game Reset Character erases your progress on this device (your purchases are kept). Notification reminders you allow are scheduled on the device. The game has no analytics service, and no usage data leaves your device.

Cloud backup (optional)

Only when you tap Back up or Restore (on iPhone and iPad, Back up to iCloud or Restore from iCloud) does the game write or read a copy of your save: in your own Google Play Games saved games on Android, or in your own iCloud private storage on iPhone and iPad. Google or Apple stores it for you, and we never receive it. To delete it, remove Swordpile's data from your Google account's Play Games data, or open iPhone Settings, tap your name, then iCloud, then Manage Account Storage (Storage on some versions), and choose Swordpile. The app asks your phone to leave the save out of the phone's own backups.

Advertising

The app shows a banner and optional rewarded videos from Google AdMob, set to non-personalised ads. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's software collects your IP address (which gives your approximate location), device identifiers (such as the advertising ID, app set ID or IDFV), ad interactions (ads seen, taps, video views) and diagnostics (crashes, launch time, performance). Google may link some of this to your device. Google is an independent controller of this data: see Google's privacy policy and how Google uses data from apps that use its services. We send Google nothing about you or your gameplay, and we do not track you across other companies' apps. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent message asks you first; without consent you get limited ads that use no identifiers. Change your choice at any time from Privacy Choices in the More menu.

Purchases

Google Play or the App Store handles payment; we never see your payment details. When purchase checking is on, the game sends the product and the store's receipt (Google's purchase token, or Apple's signed transaction) to our server, which confirms it with Google or Apple. We keep a one-way hash of the receipt, the product, when it was checked, any refund notice from the store, and a one-way hash of a random code the app made when installed (not your device or advertising ID). We keep these records for 24 months. We never store the receipt itself, your name or an account.

Rankings (optional)

Nothing is sent until you tap Join in More, then Rankings. If you join Rankings, the game sends a random code made on your device (our server keeps only its one-way hash), your best wave in each dungeon, your deepest Abyss floor, your play time, the game version and your device clock. The server gives you a random name and shows it with your positions to other players. Leave deletes your entry at once; Reset Character also deletes it. Both need a connection. Otherwise an entry is deleted 180 days after its last update (also after an uninstall). Abuse checks keep a note for 90 days. If we hide a code for cheating, we keep only its one-way hash while the hide stands. A save export contains your code, so keep exports private.

Your network address

Our server uses your IP address only to limit abuse. Our database keeps it only in scrambled form: in a request counter deleted after about a minute, and in a daily join limit whose scrambling key is deleted after two days, after which it cannot be traced back. Supabase's own request logs record the address and keep it for up to 7 days.

Emails to us

If you email us, for example with the in-game bug report, we receive your email address, your message and the device details shown in the report. We use them only to answer you, and delete them within 24 months of your last message.

Age signals

We read the age range the app store provides, where the law requires it, and use it only to apply store-required restrictions.

Why we use data (EEA and UK)

  • Ads: your consent, given in Google's consent message.
  • Rankings and abuse limits: our legitimate interest in running the optional rankings you joined and keeping them fair and secure. You can object at any time by tapping Leave.
  • Purchase check: our contract with you (delivering what you bought) and our legitimate interest in preventing fraud.
  • Emails: answering your request.
  • Age signals: legal obligation.

We make no automated decisions about you that have legal or similar effects.

Who receives data, and where

Google (AdMob, Play Games, Play Billing) and Apple (App Store, iCloud) under their own privacy policies; and Supabase, our hosting provider, which processes data for us in Frankfurt, Germany, under a data processing agreement. Your Rankings name and positions are visible to other players. Supabase and its sub-processors, and we ourselves, may access data from outside the EEA; those transfers rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. Google relies on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses.

Your rights and how to delete

You can ask us to access, correct, delete, export or restrict data about you, object to its use, or withdraw consent (this does not affect earlier use). Email us; we answer within one month. Because we hold no name or account, tell us your Rankings name so we can find your entry. To delete data yourself: Leave in Rankings, Reset Character or uninstall the app, and remove a cloud backup as described above. The Data deletion page gives the details and timelines. You can also complain to your data protection authority (EEA list, or the ICO in the UK).

Children

Swordpile is for a general audience aged 13 and up and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that a child under 13 has joined Rankings or emailed us, we delete that data. Parents can contact us with the child's Rankings name.

US state privacy rights

We do not sell your personal information or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. Where your state gives you a right to opt out, use Privacy Choices in the More menu (Google's US state message). You can ask us by email to access or delete your data, and we will not treat you differently for doing so.

Changes

If the app's data practices change, we update this policy and its effective date before the change ships.
