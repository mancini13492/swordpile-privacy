Swordpile — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 September 2026

Swordpile ("the app") is an offline idle merge game developed by Anvilbyte Studios. This policy explains what data the app does and does not handle.

The short version

Swordpile has no accounts, no sign-in, and no game server. Your game progress is stored only on your device. We do not collect, store, or sell your personal information. The app shows ads and offers optional purchases; the third-party services that provide those features process some device data, described below.

Game data

Your save (progress, settings, purchases state) is stored locally on your device. It is never uploaded. Deleting the app deletes it. The optional save-export feature produces a code you control entirely.

Advertising

The app shows a banner ad and optional rewarded videos, served by Google AdMob. We have configured ads to be non-personalized: they are selected from contextual information rather than an advertising profile. To serve ads, Google processes certain device data — including device identifiers, IP address (which implies coarse location), and ad-interaction diagnostics — as an independent provider. We do not send AdMob any information about you or your gameplay. See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses data in AdMob apps.

In the European Economic Area and the UK, a consent dialog (Google User Messaging Platform) is shown before any ads are served, as required by law.

Rewarded videos are always optional and only ever play when you press a clearly labeled button. Purchasing "Ad-Free Forge" or "Forgekeeper's Pact" removes the banner; rewarded videos remain available by choice.

Purchases

Optional purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment details. The app keeps only a record of which products you own so it can honor them. Purchases can be restored from the More menu.

Analytics

The app may record basic, anonymous usage events (for example, "a session started" or "a boss was first defeated") to help us understand whether the game's difficulty and pacing work. These events contain no personal information, no device identifiers, no location, and no free-form text, and cannot be linked to you.

Notifications

If you grant notification permission, the app schedules local reminders (for example, when your idle earnings are full). These are generated on your device; nothing is sent to a server. You can decline or revoke the permission at any time.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience aged 13 and up. It shows only non-personalized ads and collects no personal information from any user.

Your choices

  • Remove ads: purchase Ad-Free Forge, or simply never press rewarded-ad buttons.
  • EEA/UK: change your ad-consent choice from the app's settings at any time.
  • Delete your data: uninstall the app, or use the in-game Reset Character.
  • Notifications: deny or revoke the system permission.

Changes

If the app's data practices change (for example, if analytics is expanded), this policy will be updated and the effective date revised before the change ships.
