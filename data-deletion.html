Swordpile — Data Deletion

Effective date: 15 October 2026

Swordpile, by Anvilbyte Studios (operated by the developer), has no accounts and never asks for your name. This page explains how to delete what the app stores, what is deleted, what is kept and for how long. The Privacy Policy has the full picture.

In the game

  • Leave Rankings (More, then Rankings, then Leave rankings): our server deletes your Rankings entry, your name, your positions on every list and the abuse notes about your code, at once. It needs a connection; offline, nothing changes and you can try again.
  • Reset Character (More, under Save Data): erases your progress on this device. If you had joined Rankings and you are online, it also deletes your Rankings entry. Your purchases are kept on the device so they can be honoured.
  • Uninstall the app: deletes everything the app stored on the device, including your purchases list and the random codes it made. A Rankings entry you did not leave is then deleted by our server 180 days after its last update.
  • Cloud backup: Google or Apple stores it in your own account. On Android, remove Swordpile's data from your Google account's Play Games data. On iPhone and iPad, open Settings, tap your name, then iCloud, then Manage Account Storage (Storage on some versions), choose Swordpile and delete its data.

By email

Email anvilbytestudio@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data". Because we hold no name or account, include your Rankings name if you joined Rankings (it is shown in More, then Rankings). We confirm and act within one month, and delete your emails to us too if you ask.

Data held by Google and Apple

Google AdMob, Google Play and Apple handle their own data under their own policies. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings, and manage your Google data at myaccount.google.com. Purchase history kept by Google Play or the App Store is managed in your store account.
